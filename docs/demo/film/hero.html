Evergreen Notes

Andy Matuschak, a researcher who writes about tools for thought, uses the term "evergreen notes" for notes written to evolve and accumulate value over time.

That makes evergreen notes a cousin of the Feynman Technique. And rewriting an idea from memory, instead of copying it, is active recall.
