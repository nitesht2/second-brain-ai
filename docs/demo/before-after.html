A Primer on Spaced Repetition

If you review something right after learning it, you waste the review: you still remember it. If you wait too long, you have to relearn it from scratch. Spaced repetition schedules each review for the moment just before you would forget.

The idea goes back to Hermann Ebbinghaus, who measured his own forgetting in the 1880s and found that memory decays quickly at first and then levels off...

Modern flashcard software turns this into an algorithm. SuperMemo, written by Piotr Wozniak, introduced the SM-2 algorithm in 1987. Anki, a free and open-source app, uses a variant of it...

The method works best when each card makes you actively retrieve an answer rather than reread it...
