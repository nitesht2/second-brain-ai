# SuperMemo

## Summary
Early spaced repetition software by [[Piotr Wozniak]]; introduced the SM-2 algorithm in 1987.

## Key Points
- SM-2 became the basis for many later apps, including [[Anki]]

## Why It Matters
The bridge between Ebbinghaus's research and practical [[Spaced Repetition]] tools.

## Counter-arguments
- Newer algorithms improve on SM-2's fixed assumptions.

- Related to: [[Piotr Wozniak]], [[Anki]]
