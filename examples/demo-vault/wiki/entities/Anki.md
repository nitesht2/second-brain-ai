# Anki

## Summary
Free, open-source flashcard app that schedules reviews with a variant of the SM-2 algorithm.

## Key Points
- Cross-platform with sync between devices
- Intervals grow for easy cards and shrink for hard ones

## Why It Matters
The most common way to put [[Spaced Repetition]] into daily practice.

## Counter-arguments
- Easy to create too many low-quality cards.
