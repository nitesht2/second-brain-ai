# Cal Newport

## Summary
Computer science professor at Georgetown and author of Deep Work (2016).

## Key Points
- Argues focus is becoming rare and valuable
- Distinguishes deep work from shallow work

## Why It Matters
His ideas shape how and when to capture notes without breaking [[Deep Work]] blocks.

## Counter-arguments
- Advice is easier to follow for people who control their own schedule.
