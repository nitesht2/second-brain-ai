# Richard Feynman

## Summary
American physicist, Nobel laureate (1965), known for clear teaching and explanation.

## Key Points
- Famous for explaining hard physics in plain language
- The [[Feynman Technique]] is named after him

## Why It Matters
His approach to explanation is a practical test of understanding for any note you write.

## Counter-arguments
- The named technique is a later popularization, not his own formulation.
