# Niklas Luhmann

## Summary
German sociologist (1927-1998) known for his large body of work and the slip-box he used to write it.

## Key Points
- Kept roughly 90,000 linked index cards
- Described the slip-box as a conversation partner

## Why It Matters
The model for [[Zettelkasten]] practice and, indirectly, for linked-note apps like [[Obsidian]].

## Counter-arguments
- Hard to separate the system's contribution from his own expertise and discipline.
