# Hermann Ebbinghaus

## Summary
German psychologist who ran the first systematic experiments on memory in the 1880s.

## Key Points
- Tested his own memory of nonsense syllables at timed intervals
- Described rapid early forgetting that levels off over time
- Showed spaced practice outperforms massed practice

## Why It Matters
His findings are the foundation of [[Spaced Repetition]] and every flashcard scheduler built since.

## Counter-arguments
- His experiments used one subject (himself) and meaningless material.
