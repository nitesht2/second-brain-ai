# A Primer on Spaced Repetition

## Summary
Explains spaced repetition: scheduling each review just before you would forget. Traces the idea from [[Hermann Ebbinghaus]] to flashcard algorithms in [[SuperMemo]] and [[Anki]].

## Key Points
- Reviewing too early wastes effort; reviewing too late means relearning
- Spaced reviews beat massed (crammed) reviews
- SM-2 (1987) adjusts each card's interval based on how hard it was to answer
- Cards should force [[Active Recall]], not recognition

## Counter-arguments
- Spaced repetition preserves what you put in, including poorly understood material
- Card-writing quality matters more than the algorithm, and the source says little about how to write good cards
