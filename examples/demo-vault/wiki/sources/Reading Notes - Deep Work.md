# Reading Notes - Deep Work

## Summary
Notes on [[Cal Newport]]'s argument that distraction-free focus on demanding work is both rare and valuable, with the idea of [[Attention Residue]] as a cost of task switching.

## Key Points
- Deep work creates new value; shallow work is easy to replicate
- Switching tasks leaves part of your attention behind
- Schedule and protect focus blocks

## Counter-arguments
- Many roles depend on responsiveness, so long focus blocks are not always possible
- The book's evidence is partly anecdotal
