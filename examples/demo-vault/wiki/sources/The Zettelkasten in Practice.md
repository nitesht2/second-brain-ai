# The Zettelkasten in Practice

## Summary
Describes how [[Niklas Luhmann]] used a slip-box of about 90,000 linked index cards, and how the practice carries over to apps like [[Obsidian]].

## Key Points
- One idea per card ([[Atomic Notes]])
- Rephrase in your own words instead of copying
- Link every new card to existing ones; the value lives in the links
- Luhmann treated the box as a conversation partner

## Counter-arguments
- Luhmann's output also reflects decades of expertise, not just his filing system
- Cheap links in software can produce a graph full of meaningless connections
