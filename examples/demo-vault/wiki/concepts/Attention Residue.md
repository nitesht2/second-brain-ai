# Attention Residue

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Tags:** #focus
**Related:** [[Deep Work]] · [[Cal Newport]]

---

## The Idea
After switching tasks, part of your attention stays on the previous one, lowering performance on the new task.

## Why It Matters
A reason to batch shallow tasks and avoid checking messages during [[Deep Work]].

## Counter-arguments
- Effect size varies by task and person
