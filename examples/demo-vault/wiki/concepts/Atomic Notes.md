# Atomic Notes

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Tags:** #pkm
**Related:** [[Zettelkasten]] · [[Obsidian]]

---

## The Idea
Each note holds exactly one idea, so it can be linked and reused in many contexts.

## Why It Matters
Small notes make links precise: a link points to one claim, not a whole chapter.

## Counter-arguments
- Splitting too finely loses context
- Takes more effort than dumping a full summary
