## The Idea
A slip-box of small, linked notes where new ideas come from following connections.

## Why It Matters
The model for this vault: one idea per note, written in your own words, linked to what already exists.

## Counter-arguments
- Maintenance cost grows with the size of the box
- Links made without thought add noise
