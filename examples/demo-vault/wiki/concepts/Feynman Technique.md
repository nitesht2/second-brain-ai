# Feynman Technique

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Tags:** #learning
**Related:** [[Richard Feynman]] · [[Active Recall]]

---

## The Idea
Test your understanding by explaining a concept in plain language; where the explanation breaks, you have found a gap.

## Why It Matters
A quick check before writing a note: if you cannot explain it simply, you are not ready to file it.

## Counter-arguments
- Named after Feynman but not formulated by him
- Oversimplifying can hide real nuance
