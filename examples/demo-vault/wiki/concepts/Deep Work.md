# Deep Work

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Tags:** #focus
**Related:** [[Cal Newport]] · [[Attention Residue]]

---

## The Idea
Long, distraction-free focus on cognitively demanding tasks produces most of the valuable output.

## Why It Matters
Capture ideas fast during focus blocks and process them later, so note-taking supports focus instead of breaking it.

## Counter-arguments
- Not every job allows long uninterrupted blocks
