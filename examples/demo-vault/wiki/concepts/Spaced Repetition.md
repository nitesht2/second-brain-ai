# Spaced Repetition

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Tags:** #learning #memory
**Related:** [[Hermann Ebbinghaus]] · [[Anki]]

---

## The Idea
Review material at growing intervals, each timed just before you would forget it.

## Why It Matters
Turns one-time reading into long-term memory for the small set of facts you actually need to keep.

## Counter-arguments
- Only preserves what you put in; it does not create understanding
- Daily review habits are hard to sustain
