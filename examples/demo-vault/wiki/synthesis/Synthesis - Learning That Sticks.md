# Synthesis: Learning That Sticks

## Overview
These entries cover how memory works and how to work with it: the research behind forgetting, the tools that schedule reviews, and the habits that make reviews effective.

## Shared Patterns
- Effortful retrieval beats passive review (seen in [[Active Recall]] and [[Feynman Technique]])
- Timing matters as much as effort (seen in [[Spaced Repetition]], [[Hermann Ebbinghaus]], [[SuperMemo]])

## Contradictions Found
- [[Spaced Repetition]] optimizes keeping facts, while [[Feynman Technique]] targets understanding - resolution: explain first, then turn only what you understand into cards.

## Key Insight
Every method here is a way of making yourself produce knowledge instead of re-reading it. Schedulers like [[Anki]] decide when; explanation and recall decide whether the review does anything.

## Actionable Takeaways
- Before making a flashcard, explain the idea in one plain sentence
- Review new notes within a day, then let the intervals grow
